1. What do you think this tool is trying to help you do?
I think it's designed to remind people to save their work so they don't lose progress if their computer crashes or something goes wrong. The pet makes the reminder feel more personal than a regular notification.

2. What part of the interface felt most clear?
The pet and its message were the clearest parts. It was easy to understand that it was trying to get my attention and remind me to save my work. The friendly appearance also made its purpose approachable.

3. What part felt confusing?
I wasn't immediately sure whether the pet was just there for decoration or if I was supposed to interact with it. It could be a little clearer when the pet is reminding me to save and when it's just idling.

4. How did the tool make you feel when it intervened?
It felt friendly and not too demanding. I liked that it didn't make me feel like I had done something wrong for forgetting to save. However, I'd want to make sure the animation doesn't become distracting if I'm focused on a task.

5. Would you realistically use something like this? (1 is no way and 5 is absolutely yes)
5 stars
